<objective>
**User story (derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line):** *As a* 10–12-jährige Handy-Besitzerin, *I want to* das Thema „Bildschirmzeit & Balance" auf meinem Handy öffnen und komplett durchlesen — zuerst Fakten, dann den Selbstcheck „Wie ist das bei dir?" mit Antworten, die zu meinem echten Alltag passen (45-Minuten-Deal, Musik zählt nicht), dann „Was kann ich tun?"-Tipps — *so that* ich mich informiert und ermutigt fühle, nie belehrt oder bewertet — und Happi dabei immer in derselben freundlichen Stimme zu mir spricht.

Purpose: This plan ships the **voice spec first** (VOICE-01 — the anti-lecture guarantee must exist as a written artifact *before* any copy is finalized, D-07, and it becomes the machine-readable source for the copy verification gate) followed by the **phase tracer**: the data-driven topic layer end-to-end. One bare-array `topics.json` + one pagination `themen.njk` emit all five `/themen/<slug>/` routes (Phase 3 then costs zero template work); the reference topic carries the complete widget markup (self-check Version A, tips, balance, next-topic cards, guards) that plans 02-02 (interactivity) and 02-03 (Happi identity) expand. This plan encodes the three build-failure pitfalls the research caught by real failures (wrapped-data pagination collapse, nested-loop radio exclusivity, eleventyComputed double-escape) and proves the subpath/escape/zero-request guarantees on the built output.
Output: `docs/stimme-und-stil.md` (gating artifact), five built topic routes (reference topic full, four „kommt bald" stubs), home page retargeted to the new single topic source.
</objective>
