<objective>
**User story (derived from the ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal line):** *As a* 10–12-jährige Leserin, *I want to* eine Selbstcheck-Antwort antippen und sofort eine freundliche Reflexion dazu zu sehen bekommen — ohne Knopf, ohne Punkte, ohne Bewertung — *so that* das Beantworten sich anfühlt wie ein Gespräch mit Happi, nicht wie ein Test.

Purpose: This plan brings the widget to life (SELF-01, D-02): the pure-CSS `:has()` reveal that swaps a per-answer reflection on tap (works with zero JS — progressive enhancement), the tap-target/tip-box/balance/facts styling that makes the reference topic readable and warm, and the tiny generic `app.js` that only upgrades the interaction for screen readers (aria-live announcement). It operates on the markup 02-01 already proved in the build, so nothing architectural changes — pure expansion of the proven slice.
Output: a visually complete, keyboard-accessible, tap-friendly reference topic page whose self-check answers are never stored or transmitted.
</objective>
